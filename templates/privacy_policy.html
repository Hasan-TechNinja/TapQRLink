1 Introduction & Overview

Welcome to TapQRLink ("we," "our," "us," or the "Service"). TapQRLink is dedicated to providing high-speed, intuitive, and secure QR code scanning, barcode processing, custom QR code generation, NFC link pairing, and link management tools.

This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect when you use our mobile applications, web platform, and related APIs, how that information is processed and safeguarded, and the rights and choices available to you under applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Key Takeaway

We collect only the minimum data required to deliver QR scanning, code generation, and account synchronization services. We never sell your personal data or scan history to third parties.

2 Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with TapQRLink, we may collect the following categories of information:

Account Credentials
Email address, encrypted password, username, bio, and optional mobile number when you register an account.
QR & Scan Data
URLs, embedded text, scan timestamps, custom QR designs, and generation history tied to your account.
Device & Usage
Device model, operating system version, IP address, unique device identifiers, and crash analytics.
Push Notifications
Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration tokens to deliver account alerts, security codes, and updates.

A. Information Provided Voluntarily: When you register, verify your email, submit feedback, or upload a profile picture, you provide us with personal contact details and profile content.

B. Social Authentication: If you choose to log in via third-party providers such as Google Sign-In, we receive authentication tokens, your verified email address, name, and profile avatar as permitted by your third-party provider settings.

C. Anonymous / Guest Scanning: If you use TapQRLink without creating an account, your scans are processed locally on your device without linking to personal identities, unless explicitly saved or shared.

3 Device & Camera Permissions

To provide functional QR and barcode scanning, our mobile and web applications require specific device permissions:

Permission Purpose Data Handling
Camera Access Real-time optical scanning of QR codes and barcodes. Camera feed frames are processed in-memory in real time. We do not continuously record, stream, or store raw video/photos without your action.
Photo Library / Storage Selecting images from your gallery to decode QR codes or uploading a profile image. Only the specific photo selected by you is accessed and processed.
Push Notifications Delivering verification codes, scan alerts, subscription updates, and system notifications. Tokenized push identifiers (FCM) are stored securely on our servers. You can toggle notifications off in device settings at any time.
Internet / Network State Syncing history, resolving web URLs, validating subscriptions, and serving advertisements. Standard encrypted HTTPS network requests.
Camera Privacy Guarantee

TapQRLink activates the camera exclusively when you open the scanner viewfinder. The camera is immediately deactivated when you exit the scanner screen.

4 How We Use Your Information

We process your personal data based on legitimate business purposes, contract fulfillment, and legal compliance:

  • Service Provision: Generating customized QR codes, managing URL links, syncing history across devices, and decoding scanned barcodes.
  • Authentication & Security: Verifying email addresses via 6-digit verification codes, managing secure JWT sessions, processing password resets, and protecting against unauthorized access.
  • Customer Support & Feedback: Addressing bug reports, responding to user feedback, and improving app performance.
  • Subscription Management: Managing free and premium subscription statuses, renewal dates, and billing access control.
  • Fraud & Abuse Prevention: Detecting phishing URLs, malicious code payloads, and preventing automated spamming.

5 Third-Party Services & Advertising

We collaborate with reputable third-party infrastructure and service providers to maintain reliability, security, and support our free tier:

  • Google Firebase & FCM: Utilized for cloud messaging, push notification routing, and crash diagnostics.
  • Google AdMob & Ad Partners: We display banner and interstitial ads for non-premium users. Advertising partners may collect non-personal device identifiers and cookies to serve relevant advertisements in accordance with Google's advertising policies and industry standards (including app-ads.txt and ads.txt compliance).
  • Google Sign-In: For frictionless single sign-on authentication.
  • Transactional Email Services: For sending security codes, account verifications, and administrative notices.

All third-party service providers are bound by strict confidentiality and data protection agreements and are restricted from using your data for any independent purposes.

6 Data Storage & Retention

We retain your personal data and QR history for as long as your account remains active or as required to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy.

  • Scan & Generation History: Kept in your account until you delete individual records or request account deletion.
  • Verification & Reset Codes: Expire automatically within 2 to 10 minutes of issuance and are purged regularly.
  • Inactive Accounts: Accounts inactive for extended periods may be queued for automated anonymization or deletion after prior notice.

7 Your Rights & Account Deletion

Regardless of your geographic location, TapQRLink grants you full control over your personal information:

  • Access & Portability: You have the right to request a copy of the personal information and scan records we hold about you.
  • Rectification: You can edit your profile information, email, or mobile number directly through the app settings.
  • Deletion ("Right to Be Forgotten"): You can permanently delete your scan history or complete account at any time. When you request account deletion, all associated scan history, notifications, profile images, and authentication records are permanently erased from our production servers.
  • Opt-Out of Push Notifications: You can disable notifications via your mobile device system settings or app preferences.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at help@tapqrlink.com or use the in-app account deletion utility.

8 Security Standards

We employ industry-leading security practices to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS): All network communications between your device and our servers are encrypted using modern cryptographic protocols.
  • Password Hashing: Passwords are protected using robust cryptographic algorithms (e.g., PBKDF2 with SHA-256) with salted hashes. Plaintext passwords are never saved or visible to staff.
  • JWT Token Authentication: Session tokens are securely generated with short expiration windows and automated refresh mechanisms.

9 Children's Privacy

TapQRLink is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions within the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information without verified parental consent, we will promptly delete the data from our records.
